About us

Safety that lives on the job site

SacredOps gives construction and warehouse contractors an organized, audit-ready way to run field safety and daily operations — on the phones your crew already carries.

Why we built it

The binder was never the answer.

Field safety runs on paper — sign-in sheets, permits, toolbox talks, SDS binders, inspection forms. It gets filled out in the field, then lost in a truck, a filing cabinet, or an inbox. When an inspector shows up, or an incident happens, the record you need is exactly the one you can't find.

Bigger companies solve this by hiring a full-time safety director and buying enterprise software built for enterprise budgets. Small and mid-size contractors get left with binders and spreadsheets — doing the same real work, with none of the tools.

SacredOps was built to close that gap: put the whole safety operation on the crew's phones, roll every signed and dated record up to a supervisor dashboard in real time, and make it affordable enough that a one-crew contractor can run it on day one.
