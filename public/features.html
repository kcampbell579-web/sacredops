Compliance & safety

Toolbox Talks & Sign-Ins

Assign the day's talk, collect crew signatures on their phones, and export a signed PDF — attendance proven, not promised.

SDS Library

Every safety data sheet on hand — GHS pictograms, hazards, first-aid, PPE and disposal — searchable in the field.

OSHA Board

Required postings with plain-language summaries and worker acknowledgment — the compliance board that lives in a pocket.

Forms & Permits → PDF

Confined space, hot work, equipment checklists, competent-person and more — fill, sign on screen, and file a clean PDF.

Inspections & Checklists

Color-coded checklists with corrective actions tracked to closure — with a QR sticker for any piece of equipment.

Incident Reports

Automatic tracking number, a tap-the-body injury map and a full OSHA-style PDF — filed from the site in about a minute.

JSA / JHA Generator

Build job hazard analyses from an editable task library, set risk levels, and generate a numbered, shareable JSA.

Training & Certification Tracker

Every worker's cards and clearances shown as valid / expiring / expired — so nothing lapses into a citation.

Personalized HASP / HazCom

Company-specific written safety programs built around your policies and projects.

Enterprise
Field operations

Multi-Site Attendance

GPS sign-in and a live "who's on site" view across every job — no more calling foremen for a headcount.

Daily Logs

Capture the day's events, weather, deliveries and notes on each project — a running record you can pull anytime.

Expense Tracking

Job-cost expenses with receipt photos, captured in the field and ready for accounting.

Certified Payroll

Hours, overtime and fringe captured on site and exported accounting-ready — for prevailing-wage and public work.

Look-Ahead Scheduler

Plan a 6-week look-ahead and export a clean schedule PDF to share with the crew and the GC.

Subcontractor Management

A sub directory with COI tracking and shared project access via a matching contract number.

Enterprise
Oversight & reporting

Live Dashboards

Crew on site, sign-in %, open inspections and compliance status across every job — the whole operation at a glance.

Reports Dashboard

Roll up submissions, inspections, incidents and attendance into cross-project reporting management can use.

CSV Export

Pull any report to CSV for your own spreadsheets, accountant, insurer or GC — your data stays yours.

Portfolio Oversight

Every active project in one view — which are current, which have open items, and where follow-up is needed.
